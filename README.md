📊 BrightCart E-Commerce Analytics

End-to-End Business Intelligence Project using SQL, Power BI, and Business Reporting

📌 Project Overview

BrightCart is a simulated e-commerce company seeking to understand the key drivers of profitability across products, sales channels, and marketing platforms.

The objective of this project is to transform raw transactional and marketing data into actionable business insights using SQL for analysis and Power BI for interactive visualization.

The project focuses on identifying:

Most profitable product categories
Cost drivers reducing profit margins
Product return behavior
Sales channel performance
Marketing platform effectiveness
Campaign ROI optimization
🎯 Business Questions

This analysis answers seven business questions:

Which product categories generate the highest profit?
What costs reduce profitability the most?
Which categories suffer the highest return rates?
Which sales channels perform best financially?
Which sales channels experience the greatest revenue loss from returns?
Which marketing platforms deliver the highest ROAS and acquisition efficiency?
Which campaigns should be reduced first to maximize marketing ROI?

📊 Key Findings
Product Categories
Electronics generated the highest profit margin (26%).
Books produced the lowest profitability despite moderate sales volume.
Shipping costs significantly reduced margins for Books and Beauty products.
Returns
Electronics experienced the highest return rate (9%).
Website sales generated the greatest revenue loss from returned products.
Social Commerce showed the highest percentage of returned orders.
Sales Channels
Mobile App delivered the highest profit margin.
Website generated the largest revenue.
Marketplace channels showed lower profitability due to platform fees.
Marketing
TikTok Ads achieved the highest ROAS.
Influencer marketing delivered strong returns with moderate spending.
Email Marketing had the weakest performance across ROAS, CPA, and CPC.
💡 Business Recommendations
Product Strategy
Reduce shipping costs for low-margin categories.
Review pricing strategy for Books and Beauty products.
Improve return prevention for Electronics.
Sales Channels
Increase investment in Mobile App.
Optimize Website return policies.
Reassess Marketplace profitability.
Marketing
Increase budget allocation to TikTok Ads and Influencer campaigns.
Reduce spending on Email Marketing.
Continuously monitor campaign ROI to eliminate low-performing campaigns.
